## Strategic implications

**The diagnosis is not "AI invisibility" — it's "category authority gap."** Alex's site is fully indexable, well-structured, and owns his name. What he doesn't own is:

- Any defensible category term (cognitive debt is gone; need a new one)
- Any community presence (Reddit, HN, Crunchbase)
- Any positioning on the head money keyword that VCs/founders actually search

This means the AEO/GEO research finding from 2026-04-19 ("content patterns inside posts drive AI citations") is necessary but not sufficient. Without entity-presence in the third-party graph (Reddit threads, HN comments, Crunchbase, listicle inclusion), even perfect content won't get cited because AI training data weighs cross-platform signals heavily.

# Remote Tech Income Research — Non-Senior Roles, Degree Filter, Solar Lane, Income Stacking (2026-08-17)

**Status:** CURRENT
**Session:** Second research sweep (5 agents) after pivot: goal is consistent remote income ($50-90k equivalent) while building the business — broad tech roles, not senior-dev/executive. Companion: `tech-resume-best-practices-2026.md` (mechanics still apply), vault `Career/resume-intake.md`.

## Key Findings

### The degree filter — Alex's theory is half right, and the half that's wrong helps him

- **Degree requirements ARE tightening as a volume filter:** Indeed Hiring Lab's occupation-adjusted series rose from 16.6% (Nov 2023) to 19.3% (Nov 2025) of postings requiring a bachelor's — same job titles re-adding the line. Hiring Lab explicitly calls it "a shortlisting mechanism." Driver: ~242 applications per opening, ~34% auto-apply/AI-assisted.
- **But skills-based hiring is mostly theater anyway:** HBS/Burning Glass (11k+ postings, 9 years) — 45% of companies that "dropped degree requirements" changed nothing; fewer than 1 in 700 hires benefited. Only ~37% genuinely converted (Apple, Walmart, Target named). The JD's degree line is weak signal in either direction.
- **The real gate is the ATS knockout dropdown, not resume parsing.** ~92% of rejections are manual or eligibility-knockout (degree Y/N, work auth, required cert); only ~8% of ATS deployments do content-based auto-rejection. Consequence: resume keyword optimization is near-worthless against the dropdown; channels that put a human first are worth disproportionate effort.
- **The actual dominant 2026 screen is AI fluency: 75-79% of US tech postings require an AI skill** (Dice, up 178% YoY). Fastest-growing named skills: enterprise integration +638%, agentic AI +587%, AI agents +503%, vector databases +353%, prompt engineering +253%. **This is nearly a description of Alex's stack.** Lead with these, not "full-stack developer" (the most flooded phrase in the market).
- **Reframe:** Alex is being mis-modeled as a no-degree job seeker. The solar company work is *paid employment history* building internal tools/systems/automations. Titled and quantified properly, this is an ordinary experienced-hire search where the degree line is a soft preference.
- **Certs are knockout-passers, not hiring signals.** Worth buying only to unlock a specific dropdown: CompTIA Security+ (gov/DoD-adjacent), AWS Solutions Architect Associate (not Cloud Practitioner — "almost zero weight"), Salesforce Admin ($107.5k Robert Half midpoint, postings doubled), NABCEP PV Design Specialist (solar). Education section: never omit — retitle "Education & Certifications" and fill with certs; blank reads as concealment.
- **Degree-free structural lanes:** MA state government (EO 627, Jan 2024: >90% of state postings have no degree requirement); federal GS-2210 IT series converting to skills-based (OPM Merit Hiring Plan — politically volatile, verify volume); staffing-agency contract/C2H (Robert Half: 66% of tech HMs adding contractors H2 2026 — intake is a recruiter conversation, no dropdown).

Baseline conversion math (242 apps/opening, ~40:1 app-to-interview) does NOT apply to portfolio-screened roles: ten artifact-backed applications beat 200 generic ones. ### The solar lane — viable ONLY if he switches segments

- **Residential (his segment) is in freefall:** 25D credit expired Dec 31 2025; installs -20% (Ohm); workforce 464k → ~280k; Enphase/Freedom Forever/Purelight/TriSMART cuts. Do not target residential designer headcount.
- **Utility-scale/C&I is setting records:** 43.4 GW in 2026 (+60% YoY), 51% of all new US generating capacity, data-center demand driven; labor is the binding constraint. The move is lateral: residential → C&I/community/utility-scale support design. Toolchain switches from Aurora/OpenSolar to **PVsyst + AutoCAD/Civil 3D** (the highest-leverage 4-6 week skill add).
- **Real remote comp:** Greentech Renewables Commercial Solar Design Project Lead $80-90k; Revamp Engineering Electrical Design Engineer I PV/BESS $82-100k; aggregate "remote solar design" $54-67k band.
- **Degree norms are looser:** only 38% of solar roles require a bachelor's (IREC 2024 Census); 74% require experience instead. Trap: "Engineer" titles re-add the EE/ME degree line; "Designer/Drafter/Specialist" titles don't. NABCEP PV Design Specialist (~$1,295 course + $500 exam) is the diploma substitute in this niche.
- **Design-outsourcing vendors (GreenLancer etc.):** growing but drafting is offshore at $20-65/hr; the defensible US-remote seats are PE-review/AHJ/NEC interpretation/QA — or the ops/tooling layer (his hybrid profile: solar orgs under margin pressure buying n8n/Make automation for plan-set intake, proposals, permitting, interconnection paperwork).
- **Solar SaaS (Aurora) is a weak bet** — repeated layoffs (Jan 2026 WARN 58 + ~100 Feb 2026); its customers are the collapsing segment. OpenSolar hiring; opportunistic only.
- **Non-obvious best $/hr fit: AI labs paying ~$85/hr for remote, async, part-time solar-domain expert work** (Handshake AI + AuraOne "Solar Design Engineer" evaluation roles, live on Indeed among ~8 remote solar designer listings; Mercor $90-120/hr band). 20 hrs/week ≈ $88k/yr, no degree screen, leaves half the week for the business. Verify rates on-platform; gig-duration risk applies.
- **MA angle:** SMART 3.0 live (PY2026 apps opened Jan 1) — C&I/community-solar shops with MA pipelines value someone who knows MA AHJs + SMART mechanics; also a consulting wedge for his own business.

### Income-stacking architecture

- **Two-leg stack beats gig portfolio.** Leg 1 (floor): ONE part-time/fractional contract — 20 hrs/wk at $45/hr market average ≈ $3.9k/mo; fractional engineering $90-130/hr ≈ $7.8k/mo — via gofractional.com, part-time filters, or staffing agencies (Robert Half/TEKsystems: skills-screened, no dropdown, C2H converts degree question into performance question). Leg 2 (upside): premium project work sold as **"AI agent systems with evals and error handling" — never "n8n workflows"** (AI-agent framing carries 2-3x budgets: $2-4k vs $600-900 for identical routing work; Fiverr commodity floor $45-520/workflow).
- **Platform reality:** Upwork = variable 0-15% fee (~10% typical), $0.90-2.40/proposal, 2-6 weeks to first contract, 90-day dropout cliff, months 1-2 are unpaid CAC; median web-dev rate $30/hr **cannot** reach $4k/mo at 62% average utilization. Degree-free access is best at the TOP: Toptal/Gun.io ($80-200/hr, code-test gated), Braintrust/Contra (0% fee but use as billing rails for self-sourced clients, not demand). The "start at Fiverr, work up" ladder is exactly backwards for this profile.
- **The n8n "$3-10k/mo freelancer" genre is infoproduct marketing** — real marketplace clearing prices are $100-300/scenario. Demand real, income claims not.
- **Overemployment reality check:** BLS multiple jobholders ~5.2%; only 23.1% of multi-jobbers are fully remote and software engineering is the *smallest* slice (2.3%). Where it works: capacity slack (25% of workers hit KPIs in ≤30 hrs) + hard separation (separate devices/accounts/calendars — most firings trace to operational failures, not policy). Same discipline applies when the second stream is his own business.
- **Tax math:** 1099 must gross 25-40% above W2 for equal take-home (15.3% SE tax, QBI 20% now permanent). $4k/mo take-home ≈ $5-5.6k/mo 1099 gross OR one 20hr W2 contract at ~$45-50/hr. Prefer one W2/agency leg as the base.
- **MA has no off-duty-conduct statute** (CA/CO/NY/ND do) — the right to run the side business must be negotiated in writing at offer stage. NLRB GC guidance (Apr 2024) makes blanket "devote full time" clauses shaky — leverage. Check IP-assignment clauses before signing.

### Overqualification handling (the other filter)

- **The objection is retention math, not skill doubt:** 75% of employers expect overqualified hires to be unmotivated, ~74% expect early exit (Harris/Express Oct 2025) — but 70% still consider them. Peer-reviewed mitigations (CMU Hahl, McGill Galperin, Personnel Review 2025): an explicit coherent "why this level, why now" story + a referral who vouches. Cold ATS is the worst channel for an overqualified profile; warm intros convert the same resume.
- **Ethical dial-down line:** retitling by function is legitimate — "Independent Consultant — Full-Stack & Automation Engineer" not "Founder/CEO" (the single highest-cost word on a mid-level application). Inventing employers/dates/metrics is not. Omission fails at the Google step (live site + LinkedIn) — include, scope down, never leave a gap.
- **One set of facts everywhere:** recruiter-side AI diffs resume vs LinkedIn; the only safe variation is emphasis and headline. LinkedIn headline swap: client-pitch language → practitioner language ("Full-stack + AI/automation engineer — React/Next.js, Python/FastAPI, self-hosted LLMs").
- **The live consulting site: reframe, don't delete.** ~73% of HMs screen online, 24% hunting for reasons to reject; a "Book a consulting call / accepting engagements" CTA tells them the business is active. Mitigations: work-first homepage, demote client-acquisition CTAs, remove "accepting clients" badges. The portfolio itself is the degreeless candidate's strongest evidence.
- **Business disclosure sequencing:** don't volunteer in screens; disclose truthfully when asked / at offer; read outside-employment + IP clauses; prefer employers whose business doesn't overlap the advisory practice. (Documented rescission case exists for an operating adjacent-field LLC.)
- **"Why this role" script that tests well:** depth-and-craft, not lifestyle/money: "After running my own practice I want to go deep on one function inside a team bigger than me — no code review and no senior peers as a solo operator." Credible and verifiable for him. Failing answers: "less stress" (burnout read), "need income" (placeholder read).
- **Salary:** MA pay transparency in force since 2025-10-29 (25+ employees must post ranges) — anchor to the posted band, target middle. Never "I'm flexible" (reinforces flight-risk read). Underpricing backfires; at-band credibility is what mitigates rejection.
- **The two filters partially cancel:** the degree filter screens credential, the overqualification filter screens excess seniority. AI/automation/integration engineering (AI postings ~134% above pre-pandemic vs overall tech ~34% below) is where both are weakest simultaneously — "overqualified generalist" converts to "rare specialist."
- **Gender nuance:** experimental work (UCSD) finds overqualified men get the flight-risk read with no charitable alternative story generated — he must supply the story explicitly, early, unprompted.

## Evidence-quality caveats

ZipRecruiter/Glassdoor bands are algorithmic estimates; job-board stats are often affiliate content; cert-ROI numbers are training-vendor marketing; the Harris/Express poll is staffing-vendor-commissioned. Strong sources: Indeed Hiring Lab, HBS/Burning Glass, IREC Census, EIA, Robert Half releases, BLS/FRED, MA mass.gov (EO 627, pay transparency, SMART 3.0), OPM Merit Hiring Plan, named live postings (Revamp, Greentech, Handshake AI). Salary bands are for targeting, not negotiation anchoring.
